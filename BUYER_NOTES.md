# Buyer Notes for CreatorLinkGuard
# Signature: Mourad.Soltani

## What is this?
A lightweight CLI tool that helps solo content creators, YouTubers, and affiliate marketers quickly audit the health of their affiliate links. It extracts URLs from descriptions or lists, checks status codes and redirects, and flags potential affiliate links. Perfect for avoiding lost commissions from broken links.

## Target Buyers
- Solo YouTube creators with affiliate descriptions
- Newsletter operators
- Indie affiliate marketers
- Small agencies managing client content

## Pricing Suggestion
- One-time purchase: $19-49 for the tool + lifetime updates
- Or freemium: free for <10 links, paid for bulk/scheduled

## Why Buy?
- Saves time vs manual checks
- Prevents revenue loss from dead links
- Simple, no-setup, local/private
- Solo-founder built, maintainable
